# Wednesday: the game behind us, the matchup ahead

Accepted September 26, 2026. This describes the next product iteration; the current builder generates the recap prototype only.

## Homepage

Open with one concise editorial headline and a featured performance supported by the week's records. Follow with participant cards and a compact, position-specific box score. Let readers move from a headline to the source evidence in two steps. Keep the account of good and poor outcomes candid and contextual.

Add an “Up next” strip grouped by team: opponent, date, kickoff in Eastern time, and venue. Players who share a team share schedule details. Each participant's weekly page has a deeper preview. Confirmed nonparticipants receive a compact availability note instead of an empty full-size card. Unknown participation gets its own label; missing statistics never become a DNP or a benching explanation.

## Player page

1. Identity, team result, participation evidence, and position-appropriate box score.
2. What changed the game: selectable key plays, field-position changes, and offensive-team EPA with its sign convention explained. Defense-view EPA can be shown only when clearly relabeled. No universal player grade or invented tracking animation.
3. Workload and season context, with sample size and missingness visible.
4. Up next: opponent, kickoff, venue, one evidence-based angle, and preliminary next-game availability.
5. Sources, retrieval/effective dates, corrections, and a link to the edition's data.

Use team-color accents, strong typography, original player monograms, and field diagrams. Logos and headshots remain disabled pending documented rights clearance. Layout must remain attractive without photographs.

## Preview evidence contract

- Resolve the next game from the latest validated schedule and current roster team; do not assume the next numeric week is the next game.
- Preserve a traded player's previous team in the recap. Explain the team change; do not imply a guaranteed role with the new team.
- Show a confirmed bye explicitly and list the next scheduled game. If the schedule cannot distinguish a bye, postponement, or an undetermined playoff opponent, say the next matchup is unconfirmed.
- Kickoff and venue remain provisional when the feed supplies no confirmed value. Name the timezone; never mistake a venue's local time for Eastern time.
- Choose one position-relevant angle from data available by the edition's cutoff. Include metric definition, games covered, denominator where applicable, and source. For example, opponent sacks allowed last week can support a pass-rush storyline, but not a prediction of one player's sacks or attribution to a single blocker.
- Omit the angle with a plain explanation when evidence is insufficient. Early-season samples cannot support confident league rankings or projections.
- Past-game injury reports describe that game's reporting period only. Next-game availability must match the next game, team, and report date. At Wednesday 9 a.m. label it “Preliminary; this week's practice reports may not yet be available.” An empty injury feed is not a clean bill of health.
- Historical replays must not use today's roster to simulate what was known then. Suppress previews unless an appropriately dated snapshot exists, or label an explicitly retrospective schedule note separately.
- After the final game, publish a recap-only edition with “Season complete” where verified; no invented next opponent.

## Publication lifecycle

Wednesday 9 a.m. Eastern is the target, including postseason. A production collection stage should run earlier, and publication should wait for completed-week validation, an archived edition, and a verified public URL. GitHub scheduling is best-effort. On essential-data failure, the default proposal is to delay the new edition and notify the owner while keeping the last good report available.

Plan a Thursday comparison for stat corrections. Preserve the original snapshot, version changed facts, display an update timestamp, and do not automatically repeat the original social post. Major public corrections need a separate, clearly labeled correction message.

Before enabling the schedule, manually validate an entire edition, its archive, mobile/player pages, social graphics, live links, and per-platform posting receipts. The social package proposal remains one roundup and one standout graphic, pending the owner's preference.
