# Media policy

Checked September 25, 2026. This records practical publication rules, not a legal opinion about every possible editorial use.

**Default: original graphics, ordinary team-name/abbreviation labels, player names and monograms, with restrained team-color accents. NFL/team logos and upstream player headshots are disabled.** Do not reproduce a team's uniform, badge, shield or distinctive brand layout. Keep the independent-publication label visible. Team colors are a design fallback, not a blanket trademark clearance.

The NFL provides no general reuse license for its logos or photographs. [NFL terms](https://www.nfl.com/legal/terms/), particularly sections 1.1–1.3.

Photo copyright, trademark rights, and rights associated with a person's likeness are different questions. An open-source package or downloadable data table containing an image URL does not establish a license for that image. Attribution, linking to a CDN, and a noncommercial portfolio purpose do not by themselves resolve the question. [U.S. Copyright Office: photographs and fair use](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-fairuse.html).

Fair use can apply to news reporting/commentary, but it requires a case-specific analysis. It is not automatic because a page is free or educational. [U.S. Copyright Office Fair Use Index](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/). This project will not treat a possible exception as a blanket permission for automated image republication across a website, LinkedIn and X.

Before enabling an image, record its exact source, photographer/rightsholder, permission or asset-specific license, attribution requirement, permitted web/social uses, modification restrictions and expiration date. Prefer images supplied with explicit permission or an asset-specific open license that supports the intended use. Player consent alone may not clear the photographer's copyright. Do not invent synthetic athlete likenesses as a shortcut.

Upstream photo/logo URLs can remain as provenance metadata; they are not displayed by default. An IMG Academy logo requires a separate decision and permission record. The project's MIT license applies to code, not third-party media.

## Wordmark

The site's name, IMG Academy → NFL, is set as plain text beside an original path graphic. It uses no IMG Academy or NFL logo, typeface or trade dress, and the masthead states that it is not an official IMG Academy or NFL publication. The author works at IMG Academy, so the site never claims to be unaffiliated; it is a personal project, not an official publication. Using an official IMG Academy mark requires written permission recorded here first: the grantor, scope, permitted web and social uses, attribution and any expiry. The site's colors follow the official IMG Academy Brand Guidelines (v1.2, May 2024); colors alone do not imply endorsement, and the independence label stays in the masthead.
